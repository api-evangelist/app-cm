> Tokyo-based operator of App-CM, a proprietary smartphone video-advertising delivery

> algorithm to play video advertising smoothly in mobile environments. Also develops
> and publishes consumer mobile apps (FriendQuiz, SunQ, Shakin). Backed by 500 Global.

## What an agent should know first

App-CM publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no machine-readable specification,
and no publicly distributed SDK**. A publisher-side iOS/Android video ad SDK does exist —
the FAQ says publishers integrate the ad unit via SDK, and that a publisher running its own
video ad system can serve App-CM inventory without it — but it is obtained only by
contacting sales. There is no reference to read, no spec to fetch, and no package in npm,
PyPI, CocoaPods, Maven Central, or a public GitHub organization.

Do not attempt to construct API calls against app-cm.co.jp. No API host exists:
`api.app-cm.co.jp`, `developer.app-cm.co.jp`, `docs.app-cm.co.jp` and `sdk.app-cm.co.jp`
do not resolve in DNS, and `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs` and
`/llms.txt` all return 404 on the corporate host. Every `/.well-known/` discovery path
(security.txt, openid-configuration, oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog,
ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json, agent.json) returns 404.

The correct action for any integration intent is to use the contact form.

## Services

- [App-CM for publishers](https://www.app-cm.co.jp/service/media.html): impression-based
  monetization for iOS/Android apps. Two ad formats — interstitial (320x250) and movie
  feed (in-feed / rectangle, min 200px wide, 16:9).
- [App-CM for advertisers and agencies](https://www.app-cm.co.jp/service/ad.html):
  auto-play in-view video with viewable-impression guarantee, delivered across a curated
  network of apps with 1M+ downloads and mobile media with 1M+ monthly pageviews.
  Packaged audience buys plus brand-lift measurement (patent pending) and post-campaign
  effectiveness reporting.
